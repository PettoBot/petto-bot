# Changelog

All notable changes to Petto are documented here.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Feeds: music is not only about one artist.** On Deezer, Apple Music and Spotify, **playlists** (Deezer and Spotify: the songs added to them are posted), **charts** (Deezer by genre, Apple Music by country: a song or album that enters the top 10 is posted; type `top rock` or `top colombia` in the dashboard) and, on Spotify, the **new albums of a country** (`new colombia`). The link of a **song or an album** follows its artist. Apple Music playlists are not possible (Apple does not let bots read them). The search of the dashboard finds public playlists next to the artists.
- **Feeds: lists of what is new.** Modrinth can follow the newest projects of a kind (`new mods`, `new shaders`, modpacks, resource packs, plugins, data packs) and Steam the lists of its store (`new` releases, `top` sellers, `coming soon`), next to the accounts and games it already followed. These lists can be busy.
- **Feeds: random music, and more animals with more sources.** A new auto feed, `randommusic`: a song picked at random from the charts of a random genre of Deezer, every 5 minutes up to once a day, with the cover card of `!deezer`. The animals have **birds, ducks, capybaras and bunnies** next to cats, dogs and foxes, and every animal has several free services: one is used for each time slot and the next one answers when it is down. `animals` mixes all of them.
- **Feeds: the names of the lists speak your language.** The charts, the new albums of a country and the lists of Steam and Modrinth are named in English, Spanish or Brazilian Portuguese (the language of who follows them), and so are the labels of the search of the dashboard.
- **Feeds: what the bot learned about YouTube survives a restart (Redis).** With the Redis of Discloud (`DISCLOUD_REDIS_URL` or `REDIS_URL`) the bot keeps the videos known not to be live, the streams that were on (so the recording of one that ended is never posted as a new video), the length of each video and the units of the YouTube key spent today, and reads them back when it starts: a restart does not ask about every recent video of every channel again and the count of the day is the real one. Without Redis it works from memory as before. Ten thousand pushed channels are subscribed in about an hour and the look is 600 channels a minute.
- **Feeds: one feed for all the animals.** The cats, dogs, foxes, birds, ducks, capybaras and bunnies are no longer a platform each: there is one, `animals`, that posts any of them or only the ones asked for (`!feed add animals 15m cat dog`, in English, Spanish or Portuguese: `gatos perros`). Each choice is a feed of its own. The feeds of the single animals that were already made keep working.
- **Feeds: random music from Deezer, Apple Music and Spotify.** `randommusic` takes a song from the charts of a random genre of Deezer, from the top 100 of a random country of Apple Music or, when the bot has its Spotify keys, from a random search of Spotify: a different service in each slot, the next one when one does not answer. Spotify now says in the log what it answered when it fails (and a wrong id or secret is told apart from a Spotify that is down).
- **Feeds: Modrinth lists in one feed, and they are posted.** `new:mod+shader` follows the newest projects of several kinds in one feed (`new:all` for every kind), so a server does not use its places on one kind each. The lists of new projects were never posted because their kind of notice was not one a feed had chosen: they are now `project` notices ("New project"), and the feeds made before get them too.
- **Feeds: YouTube videos and Shorts can show the player.** A new option of a feed, `player`: the bot posts the link (with the words of the feed) instead of the card, and Discord draws the playable video, like a person pasting it. Only for the videos and Shorts of YouTube; streams and everything else keep their card. The dashboard has a switch for it under *More options*.
- **Feeds: a pushed YouTube video does not wait half an hour.** After a push the channel is looked at again at 1.5, 5, 10 and 20 minutes (the feed of a channel can take that long to show a new video), and a channel that pushes is now looked at every 10 minutes (every 30 only when more than 1500 channels are followed). A video that reaches the notice more than 8 minutes after it was published says so in the log, and whether a push arrived for it, to tell a late push from one that never came.
- **Feeds: the YouTube search of the dashboard costs less.** A name written as one word is tried as an `@name` first (one unit) before the search of a hundred units; the answers are kept a day; a server has 10 searches of a hundred units a day (60 for all); and the dashboard waits a second without typing (and three letters) before it looks for a YouTube name.
- **Database: what Petto stored for a server it left is deleted 3 days later.** The date a server removed Petto is written down (`guild_departures`); if the server adds Petto again before 3 days, nothing is lost. After that, its settings and everything tied to it (about 190 tables, also the code data of custom commands) are deleted, 25 servers per pass, every 6 hours. A server with a Premium slot in use, or with backups kept in this database, waits. Nothing is decided while the bot is not ready or has loaded fewer servers than Discord says it is in. `GUILD_PURGE=false` turns it off, `GUILD_PURGE_DAYS` changes the 3 days and `GUILD_PURGE_DRY_RUN=true` only writes in the log what would be deleted. Servers that left before this change get their date when they are first noticed, so their deletion comes 3 days after the update.
- **Feeds: pictures by tags.** A new auto feed, `pictures` (`!feed add pictures 30m pink`): a picture of what is tagged that way, from the public feed of tags of Flickr, the same for every server that wrote the same tags at the same pace. Spanish and Portuguese words for the common colors and things are understood (`rosa` is `pink`), and some words are never followed. Pinterest has no public way to search by tag, so this is the way to have the same idea.
- **Feeds: YouTube push (WebSub), like the big bots.** The bot asks YouTube's hub to tell it when a followed channel publishes: the hub calls `https://<VERIFY_BASE_URL>/webhooks/youtube` with the new entry (signed with a secret of each channel), the channel is looked at at once and the notice goes out within seconds, with no quota spent on asking. A channel that pushes only needs a look every 30 minutes (to catch what a push missed); one with a stream announced or on keeps the 5-minute look, because the start of a stream is not always pushed; and when push does not work (no public address, the hub is down, a subscription ran out) everything goes on exactly as before, looking at each channel every 5 minutes. Subscriptions last 5 days and are renewed a day before. The log says once an hour how many followed channels reach the bot by push and how many units of the YouTube key were used today. `YOUTUBE_WEBSUB=false` turns it off and `YOUTUBE_WEBSUB_URL` sets another public address.
- **Feeds: the YouTube key keeps inside its daily units.** Every call to YouTube is counted (10,000 units a day by default, `YOUTUBE_DAILY_QUOTA` if Google gave more) and the log says when the day passes 80% and 95%. The search of the dashboard (100 units) stops at half of the day and the way out when the feed of a channel fails (one or two units, the length of a video is asked only once) at 70%, so the live check, which makes every notice possible, never runs dry.
- **Feeds: dogs and a mix of animals, every 5 minutes.** Two new auto feeds, `dog` and `animals` (a cat, a dog or a fox, a different one each time), next to `cat` and `fox`. All the auto feeds (Pokémon, Wikipedia, Chess.com and the animals) can now post as often as **every 5 minutes** (`!feed add dog 5m #perritos`). The animal notices no longer say where the picture comes from, it is only the picture.
- **Feeds: `/feed add` lists the platforms while you type.** Discord shows 25 choices at most and there are more platforms than that, so the platform is now picked from a list that filters as it is typed.
- **Feeds are open to every server.** They no longer need `FEEDS_PUBLIC=true`; `FEEDS_PUBLIC=false` limits them again to the owner, the developers and `FEEDS_TESTER_IDS`.
- **Feeds: follow an account and post what it publishes.** `!feed add <platform> <account> [#channel]` follows a YouTube channel (videos, Shorts and live streams), a Twitch channel (live), a Kick channel (live), a Bluesky account (posts), a Modrinth project (new versions), a subreddit (**Reddit**), a **Steam** game (its announcements), a **GitHub** repository (releases), an **npm** or **PyPI** package (new versions), an **anime on AniList** (new episodes), a **Pinterest** profile or board (pins), **any blog or news site by its RSS or Atom feed**, or the things of the day: the **Pokémon**, the featured article of **Wikipedia** and the puzzle of **Chess.com**; `!feed list`, `remove`, `test`, `pause`, `resume`, `message` and `ping` manage them (Manage Server; also `/feed`). Each notice is a Components V2 card (`src/feeds/card.js`): the title as a link with the emoji of the platform, the picture of the account at the right, the description in small text when there is one, a separator and the cover, in the color of the platform, with an optional line above it for a ping and the words of the server (`{feed.title}`, `{feed.url}`, `{feed.author}`, `{feed.description}`). A Short has no big picture unless asked, and a stream that ends is edited in place (grey, with the channel and replay buttons). A new feed first marks what the account already has as seen, so nothing from the past is posted; what is posted is remembered (`feed_seen`), so a restart posts nothing twice; after a long silence only the newest three are posted. **Limits, for each platform: Free follows up to 5 accounts of YouTube, 5 of Twitch, 5 of Reddit… and Premium up to 10 of each.** Only the team and the testers (`FEEDS_TESTER_IDS`) can use the feeds while they are tested; `FEEDS_PUBLIC=true` opens them to every server. Made for many servers: the same account followed by many servers is one request (YouTube and Bluesky are asked every 5 minutes, Twitch every 2 minutes for 100 accounts at a time, Kick every 2 minutes for 50, Modrinth, Reddit, Steam and RSS every 10, GitHub, npm, PyPI, AniList and Pinterest every 15; GitHub is asked with the tag of the last answer so a repository with nothing new costs nothing of its limit, and AniList asks for all the anime at once). A link typed for an RSS feed is only fetched from public addresses (never the bot's own network), the work is spread over the minutes, a platform that fails is asked less and less often, and a feed that cannot post five times in a row is turned off. YouTube streams need the `YOUTUBE_API_KEY` the bot already has for `!youtube` (one call per 50 new videos, and what is known not to be live is never asked again); Twitch needs `TWITCH_CLIENT_ID` and `TWITCH_CLIENT_SECRET`; Kick needs `KICK_CLIENT_ID` and `KICK_CLIENT_SECRET` (an app made in kick.com > Settings > Developer). The dashboard has its routes (`src/web/feedRoutes.js`) to look up an account, follow it, change a feed, post a test and preview the card. New tables `feeds`, `feed_seen` and `feed_live`, and `scripts/check-feeds.js`.
- **Feeds: search while you type.** The dashboard looks for the accounts that match what is typed (YouTube, Twitch, Bluesky, Steam, GitHub, Modrinth, npm and AniList; the others answer with the account of a link or a name) and shows them with their picture to choose one (`POST /api/dashboard/guild/:id/feeds/search`, up to 40 a minute for each server, kept five minutes; YouTube searches cost 100 units of its daily quota, so they are capped at 60 a day and a link or an `@name` is looked up instead).
- **Feeds: seven new platforms.** **Spotify** (new releases of an artist and new episodes of a podcast; it needs `SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID` and `SPOTIFY_CLIENT_SECRET`), **Deezer** and **Apple Music** (new albums and singles of an artist; no key), **Mastodon** (any server, `@name@server`), **Tumblr**, **Letterboxd** (the diary of a person) and the **free games of Epic Games** of the week, in the language and country of each server. The dashboard searches artists and podcasts while you type. There are two new kinds of notice, `release` and `game`, and `/feed` has the new platforms.
- **Feeds: the auto feeds post every X minutes.** The Pokémon, an article of Wikipedia and a puzzle of Chess.com can post from **every 15 minutes to once a day** (`!feed add pokemon 30m`, or the new "How often" box of the dashboard; the pace of a feed can be changed later). Every server that picks the same pace gets the same notice at the same time, and the services are asked once for each slot, not once for each server. A feed made before this keeps posting once a day.
- **Feeds: cats and foxes.** Two new auto feeds, `cat` and `fox`: a picture every 15 minutes to once a day (`!feed add cat 1h #gatitos`), the same for every server that picked that pace (TheCatAPI and Random Fox, no key).
- **Feeds: a new look for the releases of music.** A new release of Spotify, Deezer or Apple Music is headed by the same picture as `!deezer` (the cover next to a panel with the title and the artist over the blurred cover), with a button that opens it. If the picture cannot be drawn the card is the ordinary one.
- **Feeds: Twitch notices also carry a stand-in picture** (as Kick's) when the preview of the stream does not exist yet, and the real one is edited in when it does.
- **Feeds: a live notice of Kick goes out within a minute.** Kick is asked every minute (it was two), and the notice no longer waits for the picture of the stream, which Kick makes a little later: it goes out at once with a picture of Kick and the same message is edited to the real picture when it exists.
- **Feeds: a stream scheduled days before is announced.** YouTube streams are asked about even when their page is old, and the notice says when the stream **began** (not when its page was made, which could show "47 minutes ago" for a stream that had just started).
- **Feeds: the limit of Premium is 25 accounts of each platform** (it was 10; Free is 5). The engine keeps the list of feeds in memory (it was read again every minute) and reads the open streams in one go, so a bot with many thousands of feeds asks the database much less. Twitch is asked every minute.

- **Statistics v2: `!stats`, the analysis of a server, with a privacy panel and a server switch.** Everything is counted per server (in GMT-5), only as numbers and never what was written, and set against the period before it:
  - **`!stats`** (`!stats 30`): six numbers with their change in percent and three line charts (messages, voice hours, active members) with the period before as a dashed line. **`!stats channels`** (top channels by messages and voice with their share, and the text channels that were quiet), **`!stats members`** (top members and how much the top tenth wrote), **`!stats heatmap`** (messages by weekday and hour, part of the day), **`!stats growth`** (joins, leaves, the members line and how long the members who left had stayed), **`!stats compare`** (every number next to the period before, and how many of every 100 messages carry a link, a file, a reply or a mention) and **`!stats @member`** (messages, voice time, days in a row, best day, place and share). It was an alias of `!summary`, which stays as it was (`!digest`, `!weekly`).
  - **Privacy.** `!stats privacy` (Components V2 panel, three languages): a button to **stop being counted** (the person is left out of every server, and the counts per member that existed about them are deleted), one to be counted again and one to delete what was counted about them. `!statsconfig` (Manage Server; `view`, `enable`, `disable`, `members on|off`, `content on|off`, `delete`): a server can count nothing, keep no counts per member, skip the shape of messages, or delete every counter (behind a button only the person who asked can press). Counts per member are deleted after 13 months and the others after two years.
  - New tables (`stats_config`, `stats_optout`, `activity_content`, `member_lifespan`), counters for the shape of messages and for how long members stayed, and the rules are read into memory so every counter can ask them. Until they were read nothing is counted. `scripts/check-stats-v2.js` checks the numbers, the six pictures in three languages, the rules and, against PostgreSQL, the delete, the pruning and the reads.
- **Shorter ways to write the lookups and some moderation commands.** `!mu Notch` is `!minecraft user`, `!ms` server, `!msk` skin, `!mh` head; `!gu`, `!gr`, `!grl`, `!gc`, `!gi`, `!gp`, `!gl` for GitHub (user, repo, release, commits, issues, pulls, languages); `!ru`, `!rav`, `!rg` (Roblox); `!stp`, `!stg` (Steam); `!tc`, `!tcl`, `!tt` (Twitch); `!yv`, `!yc`, `!yl`, `!ys` (YouTube); `!lr`, `!lta`, `!ltt`, `!ltl`, `!lwk`, `!lcp`, `!la` (Last.fm); `!dzt`, `!dza`, `!dzl` (Deezer); `!its`, `!ita`, `!itp` (iTunes); `!ami`, `!mga`, `!achar` (AniList); `!cch`, `!lic` (chess). And `!ub` (unban), `!um` (unmute), `!uj` (unjail), `!ld` (lockdown) and `!cs` (case). The old ones keep working; `scripts/check-short-aliases.js` checks that none is the name or the alias of another command.
- **A picture for songs, albums and artists.** `!deezer` (track, album, artist), `!itunes` (song, album) and `!lastfm now` draw one picture as the heading of the card: the cover on the left with rounded corners (a circle for an artist), and next to it a panel with the same cover blurred behind the title, the artist and the album. It is made with the same tools as the other image cards (`imgutils/songCard.js`), kept for ten minutes, and when it cannot be drawn the card is the plain one with the cover. The covers are downloaded with the safe image loader. `infoPayload` got a `hero` option for a picture that is the heading itself.
- **Five more lookup commands.** `!chess` (`!chesscom`, `!lichess`: ratings by mode, games, title, followers; Chess.com and Lichess), `!modrinth` (`!mr`: a Minecraft mod, modpack, resource pack, shader or plugin; start the words with `shader`, `mod`... to choose the kind), `!crypto` (`!coin`: price in dollars, change today, rank, market, from CoinGecko), `!currency` (`!cur`, `!convert`: `!currency 100 usd cop eur`; 160 currencies including COP, from ExchangeRate-API, which is credited on the card) and `!animal` (`!cat`, `!dog`, `!fox`: a random picture). None needs a key.
- **Seven more lookup commands, and more inside the first seven.** Same rules as the first ones (cards with no color, link buttons, three languages, a time limit, a cache, a cooldown, prefix only):
  - **`!deezer`** (`!dz`; `!song`): `track` (with a 30 second preview and other results), `artist` (fans, albums, most played songs) and `album` (date, genre, track list). No key. **`!itunes`** (`!apple`; `!itunesapp`, `!podcast`): `song`, `album`, `artist`, `app` (rating, price) and `podcast`. No key.
  - **`!wikipedia`** (`!wp`): the summary of an article, in the language of the server (English, Spanish or Portuguese). No key.
  - **`!anilist`** (`!ani`; `!anime`, `!manga`, `!animechar`): anime, manga and characters, with the spoilers of the description cut. Titles for adults only show in a channel marked as age-restricted. No key.
  - **`!pokemon`** (`!pkmn`, `!pokedex`): types, height, weight, abilities, base stats and the Pokédex entry (in Spanish when the server speaks it). No key.
  - **`!package`** (`!pkg`; `!npm`, `!pypi`): version, weekly downloads, license, size and the repository of an npm package or a PyPI project. No key.
  - **`!tmdb`** (`!movie`, `!tv`): a movie or a series with score, genres, release and synopsis, asked in the language of the server. Needs `TMDB_API_KEY` (the v3 key, or the v4 read token).
  - **`!minecraft server`** (`!mcserver`; `!mc hypixel.net` also works): online or not, players, version, message of the day and the icon. **`!github`**: `commits`, `issues`, `pulls`, `languages` and `contributors`. **`!roblox`**: `avatar` and `group` (by id, link or name). **`!steam games`**: the games someone played the most. **`!twitch`**: `clips` and `top` (the most watched streams). **`!youtube`**: `latest` (the newest video of a channel) and `search` (five results). **`!lastfm`**: `topalbums`, `artist` (and how many times you played them), `compare` (how alike two members' music is), and `whoknows` (who in the server listens to an artist the most, from the members who linked their name).
  - Not added on purpose: Pinterest (its API needs an approval that is rarely given), SoundCloud (registering an app needs a paid Artist Pro plan) and Spotify (since 2026 its API asks for the owner's Premium and limits the users).
- **Lookups on other services: `!minecraft`, `!github`, `!roblox`, `!steam`, `!twitch`, `!youtube` and `!lastfm`.** A new **Integrations** category of `!help`. Each one answers with a card (no color) and has a link button, in English, Spanish and Portuguese, as prefix commands like the rest of Petto.
  - **`!minecraft`** (`!mc`; `!mcuser`, `!mcskin`, `!mchead`): the UUID (with dashes), the skin model, whether there is a cape, the skin full body or the head big, and buttons to download the skin and open NameMC. It needs no key (the Mojang API); the pictures are drawn by mc-heads.net.
  - **`!github`** (`!gh`; `!ghuser`, `!ghrepo`, `!ghrelease`): a user (followers, repositories), a repository (`owner/name` or its link: stars, forks, language, license, topics) and its latest release (notes and downloads). No key; without `PETTO_GITHUB_TOKEN` GitHub allows 60 calls an hour for the whole bot, so the answers are kept 5 minutes.
  - **`!roblox`** (`!rbx`): by name, id or profile link: display name, friends, followers, groups and the face. No key.
  - **`!steam`**: by custom name, Steam id or profile link: level, games, what they are playing and the games of the last two weeks (a private profile shows only the basics). Needs `STEAM_API_KEY`.
  - **`!twitch`**: whether a channel is live, its title, game, viewers and a preview. Needs `TWITCH_CLIENT_ID` and `TWITCH_CLIENT_SECRET`; the token is asked for once and kept.
  - **`!youtube video`** (`!yt`; `!ytvideo`) and **`!youtube channel`** (`!ytchannel`): a video from its link or from words to search (views, likes, length) and a channel from its @handle, link or id (subscribers, videos, views). Needs `YOUTUBE_API_KEY`; a link or a handle costs the daily quota almost nothing, but a search costs much more, so a search can be asked every 5 seconds and the answers are kept 10 minutes.
  - **`!lastfm`** (`!lfm`; `!np` and `!nowplaying` for `now`): `link` your Last.fm name (it checks that it exists, and it works in every server), `now`, `recent`, `profile`, `topartists` and `toptracks` (by period) for you or another member who linked theirs, and `unlink`. Needs `LASTFM_API_KEY`. New table `lastfm_links`.
  - A command whose key is not set answers "not set up" without calling anything. Every lookup has a time limit, a cooldown per person, a cache, and its own message for not found, private, the service being down and a limit being reached. What strangers write (bios, descriptions) cannot ping anyone or make a link.
- **`!jumbo` (also `!bigemoji` and `!enlarge`).** Shows a custom emoji (moving ones too), an ordinary emoji or a sticker big, in a card with no color, with its name, kind, creation date and ID and a link to the original picture. Type the emoji after the command or reply to a message with an emoji or a sticker. When the server does not have it yet, the card has a **Steal** button that adds it for whoever presses it, if they may manage emojis and stickers. Lottie stickers have no picture, so they are told and not shown.
- **XP rules: two new limits.** `daily_xp_cap` is the most XP a member can earn from messages in a day (the day changes at midnight in Colombia; 0 turns it off; the daily and streak bonus come on top of it), and it holds even when several messages arrive at the same moment. `voice_alone_percent` is the share of the voice XP someone earns while they are the only person in the channel (100 changes nothing, 0 gives nothing while alone). Both are in `/level rules` and `!level rules`, in the summary it shows and in the dashboard's Rules page.
- **A level-up card in the Petto style.** The new `levelup` preset of the rank and level-up card shows the avatar, the name, a `#rank` chip, "You are now in Top X%" and a hexagon from the previous level to the new one. It can also show the member's avatar decoration and their nameplate; each one is a switch of the card. New variables: `{level_top_percent}`, `{user.avatar_decoration}`, `{user.nameplate}`, `{user.server_tag}` and `{user.server_tag_badge}`. Image cards got what this needs: layers that appear only when a value is not empty (`showIf`), layers that follow the right edge of another one (`follow`), a pill behind a text, a line limit for texts and a hexagon shape (up to 20 layers per basic card). The level-up notice now knows how many members are ranked, so the percentage is right.
- **A decoration that moves on the level-up card (Premium).** The Level-up card has a new switch, `animateDecoration`: when the member's avatar decoration is an animated one, the card goes out as an animated PNG (APNG) with the decoration moving, and only that part of the picture is stored again for each frame, so it stays small (a card of about 250 KB). It is off by default, a server without Premium keeps the still decoration, and a card that would be too big goes out still. Discord does not say whether it plays an animated PNG sent by a bot in every place, so try it before relying on it.
- **`!userinfo` and `!serverinfo` redesigned, with no color.** `!userinfo` draws the avatar decoration on the avatar of the card (as Discord shows it) and shows the server tag next to the name, with a flat bullet on each row (no 3D icons); `!serverinfo` is organized in two blocks (people and channels, about the server) and also shows the invite splash. The card editor's preview in the dashboard tells which of these the person previewing has (`X-Card-Look`).
- **`!usercosmetics` (also `!uc` and `!cosmetics`).** One picture with the row of a profile as Discord shows it: the nameplate behind, the avatar with its decoration (moving when it moves), the name and the server tag. Under it, the details of each one and links to the pictures. A member with none of them says so.
- **Thicker hexagons on the level-up card, and no server tag on it.** The two level hexagons are wider and have a thicker outline, and the server tag is not drawn by that layout (`{user.server_tag}` is still a variable for any card).
- **The cards of Petto have no color, except to say yes or no.** A card (Components V2) has no color on its side unless it is green (it went well) or red (it was refused, or it needs attention): a message that starts with the "approved" icon is green, and one that starts with the "denied", "alert" or "warning" icon is red, with no need to choose it in each place. The sanction cards keep their own colors, and so does a color a server owner picks for its panels, forms or confessions. The grey, purple, yellow and orange sides of the info commands, `!version`, the lists, the polls, the verification and ticket cards, the requests, the reports and the usage cards are gone. Appeals are green when accepted and red when rejected, and a form is green when accepted and red when denied.
- **No big titles.** The cards use `###` or **bold** for a title, never `#` or `##` (the headings of the info cards, `!version`, the case list, the honeypot panel, the setup texts and the sanction designs; the quests and the rollouts are left as they were). `!version` also lost its 3D icons.
- `check-card-colors` checks all of this, and that every type of sanction has its color, icon, bar and sentences in the three languages.
- **Flat icons only.** The colorful icons of the application (alert, denied, changelog, eyes, reload, computer, note, more, minus, magic, locked, link, bug, metal, gear, rocket, and the pixel star, alert, loading and question) are no longer used: each one was replaced by the grey, flat icon of its design (or by the green check, red cross and warning that stay), in `!version`, the cards and the messages.

### Changed

- **The lookup commands work without the subcommand word.** `!mc Notch` is the user, `!gh octocat` is a user and `!gh PettoBot/petto-bot` (or a link) is a repository, `!yt @handle` or `!yt urugodd` is a channel while a video link or several words are a search, and `!lfm` alone (or with a member) is what is playing now. Short words also work: `!lfm songs`, `!lfm me`, `!mc avatar`. A command can now give `prefixDefaultSubcommand` as a function of the words typed. The same change fixes `!roblox`, `!steam` and `!twitch` with nothing after them: they sent a report to the error channel instead of showing how to write the command.
- **The alerts and refusals have their color back.** The cards of a warning have the yellow of the warnings again (the usage card of a command written wrong or with an option missing, a help that finds nothing, the counting alerts, the reminder of an unclaimed ticket, the scam alert, a partner reply that asks for a wait, a slow ping, the restore of a backup), the red stays for a refusal and the green for a confirmation. Rank cards are made in the dashboard (Image cards) with two basic layouts and a new progress bar layer; a server without a card uses a default one, with its own color for voice.
- Anti-abuse rules (`!level rules setting value`, for example `!level rules min_chars 5`): messages shorter than a set length, links and mentions left out, and the same text again within a minute earn no XP; voice XP needs a minimum number of people in the channel and can skip muted members. New servers get 3 characters, repeat protection and 2 people in voice by default.
- **XP events** (`!level event add|remove|list`): a multiplier for a number of hours or days, for all XP, messages only or voice only, started now or later.
- **Daily bonus and streaks**: extra XP for the first activity of the day plus an extra amount for each earlier day in a row (`!level rules daily_bonus streak_bonus streak_max_days`). The streak shows in `!rank`.
- **Weekly and monthly rankings**: `!top 1 messages week` and `!top 1 messages month`, for messages and voice. They start empty by themselves each week and month.
- The level-up message can carry an image card (`!level notify card:` and `!level voice-notify card:`).
- New variables for messages and cards: `{level_next}`, `{level_progress}`, `{level_xp_current}`, `{level_xp_to_next}`, `{level_rank_total}`, `{level_streak}`, `{level_messages}`, `{level_voice_minutes}` and `{level_type}`.
- Image cards have a **progress bar** layer and a `kind` (welcome or rank). Added `scripts/check-level-command.js`, `scripts/check-leveling.js` (also checks the SQL on a database when `PETTO_TEST_DATABASE_URL` is set), `check-rank.js` and `check-xp-grant.js` to `npm run check`.
- Added **image cards**: a picture drawn for a message, with the member's name and avatar, the server's member count and any other variable. A template can point at a card and it is sent with the welcome, leave and boost messages, tickets, giveaways, level-up messages, autoresponders and `!embed send`. The basic editor (four layouts, colors, a background image, free fonts) is free; the advanced layer editor and the extra fonts need Premium, and a card that used them keeps working with its basic settings if Premium ends. Pictures can come from an https link or be uploaded in the dashboard. Links are only fetched from the public internet.
- Added `scripts/check-cards.js` and `check-card-routes.js` to `npm run check`.
- Added `!embed create name code:`: the whole embed can be written as one code, such as `{embed}&v{title: Welcome {user}&v{description: Read the rules}&v{color: #ff91c2}`, and is saved and shown at once, without the editor panel. The format follows the embed scripting other bots use: blocks joined by `&v` (`$v` is read too), `&&` between the values of a block, and text before `{embed}` or `{message: ...}` for the message text. Blocks: `title`, `url`, `description`, `color`, `thumbnail`, `image`, `author`, `footer`, `field`, `timestamp`, `button` (link buttons) and `message`. Anything unsupported is reported and left out, nothing is saved if Discord would refuse the embed, and variables such as `{user}` work inside values. Discord does not allow real line breaks in a command option, so a line break is written `{newline}`, and the blocks can be glued together or spread over lines. `!embed create` without a code opens the editor panel as before. A code with message text or buttons is saved in the dashboard's format and is edited there.
- Added a page about the embed code to `!embed vars`.
- Added `scripts/check-embed-script.js` to `npm run check`.
- Added **jail** (`!jail`, `!unjail`): a jailed member loses their roles and can only see the jail channel, where they can talk to staff. `jail setup` creates the Jailed role and the #jail channel and hides every other channel from the role; `jail user <user> [duration] [reason]`, `jail remove`, and `jail list` do the rest. The roles are saved before anything changes and given back on release, a timed jail ends by itself, a jailed member who leaves and rejoins is jailed again, new channels are hidden automatically, and a role handed to a jailed member by another bot is taken off again and kept for release. `warn escalation` can now use `jail` as its action.
- Added `!purge <amount> [user] [filter] [text]`: scans the last 1 to 500 messages and deletes those that match a member, a kind (`bots`, `humans`, `links`, `invites`, `attachments`, `images`, `embeds`, `mentions`) and some text. Pinned messages are kept.
- Added `!history [user]`: status (banned, in jail, timed out, muted), totals by kind, active warnings, staff notes, reports about the member and the latest cases, in one card.
- Added reports management: every report is numbered and stored, the report card has **Claim**, **Resolve**, **Dismiss**, **Release** and **Reopen** buttons, and `!report list`, `view`, `stats`, `block`, `unblock` and `blocklist` for staff. Reporters choose a category; servers can set a cooldown, a daily limit, a required reason, a role pinged on every report, a discussion thread per report and a DM to the reporter when a report is closed. Anonymous reports stay anonymous in every view.
- Added the **Report User** user context menu next to **Report Message**.
- Added **Report User** to the message Apps list too, so the person who wrote a message can be reported next to **Report Message**. Context menus are now keyed by name and type, so a user menu and a message menu can share a name.
- `report config` is now a settings panel with selects and switches, a limits form and a **Send test report** button that also checks Petto can post in the channel.
- Added `!roll` (`2d6+3`, `d20`, `4d6kh3`), `!choose`, and the fun commands `!8ball`, `!ship` and `!rps` in a new **Fun** help category.
- Added `scripts/check-reports.js`, `check-jail.js`, `check-purge.js`, `check-dice.js` and `check-fun.js` to `npm run check`.

### Changed

- `!embed create` (and the embed panel and the code page of `embed vars`) now name their commands with the prefix that was typed, such as `!embed edit`, when they are run from a message, instead of always showing `!embed edit`.
- An embed code can now hold several embeds (`{embed}&v{...}&v{embed}&v{...}`, up to 10). When two codes are pasted one after the other and the first lost its closing brace, the first block is closed where the next `{embed}` starts instead of swallowing it as text, and a spare closing brace is reported and skipped instead of stopping the reading.
- `!8ball`, `!rps` and `!ship` now answer with a plain message instead of an embed. `!ship` attaches a picture with both avatars over a pink roses background, a heart with the score and a bar, drawn the same way for the same pair; if the picture cannot be drawn the result is still sent as text.
- **Resolve**, **Dismiss** and **Reopen** on a report now ask for a required reason, and sending the form is the confirmation. A closing reason is shown on the report card, written in the report thread and sent to the reporter in the DM; a reopening reason is shown on the card ("reopened by …") and written in the thread.
- The report thread now follows the report: it is locked and archived when the report is resolved or dismissed, and opened again when the report is reopened.

- Roleplay responses (**Respond** / **Reject**) are now a reply to the message that holds the buttons instead of an edit of it, so the original message keeps its text and only loses its buttons. The reply never mentions anyone.
- `!report send` takes a multi-word reason without quotes; the category, ping and anonymous options go after it as `--category`, `--ping` and `--anonymous`.
- Redesigned the info commands as Components V2 cards with one shared layout (heading with picture, optional banner, sections, ID footer and link buttons): `serverinfo`, `userinfo`, `botinfo`, `channelinfo`, `roleinfo`, `emojiinfo`, `inviteinfo`, `avatar`, `banner`, `color`, `roles`, `permissions` and `invites`. Mentions inside the cards no longer ping.

- A message that is a command for the bot (`!embed create ... hi ...`, an alias, or an @mention of the bot followed by a command) no longer sets off autoresponders, so a word inside the command text does not make the bot answer on top of the command. Added `scripts/check-command-messages.js` to `npm run check`.
- Fixed live counters (`!counter`) that stopped updating. Text and announcement channels cannot keep capitals or spaces, so the stored name never matched and the same channel was renamed on every pass, past Discord's limit of about two renames every ten minutes. The renames waited in line and held up every other counter.
