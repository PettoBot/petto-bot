# Code of Conduct

Petto welcomes contributors who communicate respectfully and work in good
faith. This applies to issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, chats,
and any other project space.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior directly.
- Accept technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Protect private information and do not publish secrets or user data.
- Follow the contribution, security, and AI policies.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized misconduct,
deliberate disruption, credential sharing, malicious code, and retaliation for
good-faith reports are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove content and may restrict project
access when necessary to protect contributors or users. Reports should be
made privately through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through
a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as the available
platform permits.
